# Independent verification targets

These targets came from a read-only reviewer before implementation. They are not test results.

- Missing manager observations remain explicit and prevent comparative promotion.
- An injected provider cannot label itself live and qualify simulated evidence.
- Three hundred variants of one originating task contribute at most one independent sample per action class.
- A task and its paraphrases cannot occupy development and holdout splits.
- Two action classes cannot pool samples to reach either class's threshold.
- Flipping hidden labels changes scoring but leaves the provider packet unchanged.
- Favorable advice cannot override exhausted attempts or an unapproved action or feature.
- Packet mismatches and conflicting duplicate observations reject before scoring.
- Classification success cannot stand in for completion, runtime, interruption, or cost measurements.
- Missing answers, invalid probabilities, or unexpected model identity cannot authorize acceptance.
- A total deadline includes response-body reads, retries, and backoff. Abort stops later attempts.
- Insufficient budget prevents the next attempted request. Unknown failed-request spend stays reserved.
- Provider errors and malformed bodies cannot leak credentials into reports or stderr.
- Existing goal-alignment score and advisory-veto results remain unchanged for the same valid answers.
