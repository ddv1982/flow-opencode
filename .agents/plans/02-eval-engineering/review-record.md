# Review record

## How critique

Four independent critics reviewed the current system. All four agreed that the
paired benchmark is not decision-grade and reviewer quality is not attributable.
Three raised the common sample-floor problem, release-policy gaps, and incomplete
instruction-footprint measurement. Two raised exact-artifact provenance. One found
the inconsistent-report qualification defect, which the lead reproduced directly.

## Interrogate plan review

Four independent reviewers challenged the synthesized plan. Valid consensus
findings were fixed:

- Host metadata reconnaissance now precedes schema implementation.
- Catalog policy now exists before analysis.
- Provenance now precedes live v2 emission.
- Scored escalations and reviewer truth live inside the attempt union.
- Campaign completion and a frozen statistical policy are explicit.
- Qualification CLI and scheduled workflow cut over together.
- The human canary moved to the final phase so it cannot block reviewer and paired
  infrastructure work.
- Trend comparability permits artifact changes and fixes measurement semantics.
- Blinding targets are explicit and transcript scanning is a recorded gate.
- Failed and inconclusive decisions remain auditable.
- A focused follow-up review found six remaining contract gaps. The plan now uses
  one outcome/evidence union, evidence-specific analysis policies, opaque arm
  tokens, masked-analysis freeze records, complete decision-input digests,
  role-indexed actor requirements, and a fail-closed release workflow at cutover.
  A final reviewer pass reported no remaining blockers.

Rejected findings were preferences for replacing the current harness or making
uncalibrated reviewer and paired evidence gate immediately. Both conflict with the
grounded architecture and the published evidence limits.
